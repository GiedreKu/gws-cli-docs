gws-cli

A private, personal-use command-line tool that connects a small set of Google accounts (owned and operated by Giedrė Kučinskienė) to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs and Tasks via the Google APIs, for internal business automation (e.g. filing invoices, logging payments, transcribing call recordings into spreadsheets).

This tool is not distributed to, or used by, the general public. It is used only by its owner and a small number of accounts she personally controls.
