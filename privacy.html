Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

gws-cli is a private, personal-use tool operated solely by Giedrė Kučinskienė for her own businesses. It is not a public application, is not distributed to others, and is not used to process data on behalf of any third party outside the account owner's own organization.

What data it accesses

With explicit account owner authorization (OAuth), the tool reads and writes data in a small number of Google accounts the owner personally controls, limited to:

  • Gmail: reading messages/attachments (e.g. call-recording notification emails) and sending/drafting messages on the owner's behalf.
  • Google Calendar: reading and creating calendar events.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets: reading and writing files the owner creates, including automatically generated reports and logs.
  • Google Tasks: reading and creating task items.

How the data is used

Data is used exclusively for internal business automation: for example, extracting information from emails into spreadsheets, logging payments, or transcribing audio attachments into text stored in the owner's own Google Sheets. All processing happens on infrastructure controlled by the account owner.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. No data is used for advertising. Data stays within the owner's own Google Workspace/Gmail accounts and private servers.

Data retention

Data is retained for as long as the account owner finds it useful for her own business records, and can be deleted at any time at her discretion.
